Limitations

  • Ground truth is the catalog record, not the physical product. A value can be wrong in the catalog and still count as correct here, and an answer that is true of the real product counts as unsupported if the record does not state it.
  • The assistant is given the right product record. Retrieval mistakes, such as picking the wrong product, are not measured.
  • Questions follow fixed templates in English. Real customers phrase questions more loosely, which is likely to be harder.
  • Allergen questions exclude cases the record cannot settle, such as an empty allergen list next to an ingredient list. Those are common in practice and deserve their own study.
  • One model, one date, one sample. The rates describe this catalog and this configuration, not assistants in general.
