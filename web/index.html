Catalog assistant audit

Demo Audit results Code

Grocery stores are putting assistants in front of their product catalogs. Catalogs have gaps: a nutrition value that was never entered, an allergen list that is empty. The risk is an assistant that fills the gap from general knowledge and answers anyway.

This demo runs an assistant over 5,000 real US products from Open Food Facts. Every answer must cite the catalog fields it used, and a deterministic check sends answers whose citations don't match the record to human review. The audit measures three versions of the assistant on questions whose correct answer is known from the record, including questions the record cannot answer.
